The code license and trademark permission are separate. GNU GPLv3 permits
copying, modifying and selling covered software. It does not grant permission
to present a modified product as an official DonutMediaCenter release.

## Permitted without separate permission

- Refer accurately to DonutMediaCenter in documentation, reviews, tutorials,
  compatibility statements and attribution, including screenshots.
- Redistribute unmodified official builds with their existing name and logo,
  including for a fee, provided the listing clearly identifies you as an
  independent distributor and does not imply official support or endorsement.
  Retain all required license notices and provide the corresponding source.
- Describe an independently branded fork as "based on DonutMediaCenter",
  with a clear statement that it is unofficial.
- Use the name and logo in accurate links to the official project.

## Modified products and misleading listings

Use a distinct product name and logo for modified builds, forks, or repackaged
products that add advertisements, subscriptions, bundled software or other
product changes. Do not use the project logo as their app icon or use
DonutMediaCenter/DMC as their product identity without written permission.

Do not describe an independent product or store account as "official", imply
that DonutsDelivery maintains it, or claim endorsement that was not granted.
A truthful "based on DonutMediaCenter" attribution may remain visible.

Technical compatibility identifiers (such as package IDs, API names, historical
LocalBooru paths and migration identifiers) need not change merely because a
fork is rebranded. This permission does not authorize using them to impersonate
an official app or its update/signing channel.
