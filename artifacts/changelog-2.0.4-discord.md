# What's New in LocalBooru 2.0.4

**HEADLINE: Phone pairing now survives restarts — and your library plays in VR**

## Phone Pairing

- **Pairings persist across restarts**: approved servers reconnect automatically on Mac and Windows launch, and paired credentials stay revocable from the desktop
- **macOS keychain storage**: paired-server tokens now live in the OS keychain instead of plain settings
- **Faster, simpler pairing**: the manual device-name confirmation step is gone — approve once and connect
- **Android scanner stays front and center**: QR scanning no longer loses the camera view mid-pair

## VR Video Viewer

- **Watch any video in VR**: a new integrated viewer with circular fisheye projection, GPU-accelerated texture upload, and built-in diagnostic modes

## Reliability

- **Smoother big libraries**: faster paging in huge folders, accumulated jump navigation, and SQLite pool resilience fixes
- **Linux desktop launches use the patched WebKit runtime they ship with**, avoiding mismatched system-library playback issues
